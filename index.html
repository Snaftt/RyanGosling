Life History

  • Ryan was born on November 12, 1980 in London, Ontario.
  • The son of Thomas Gosling and Donna Gosling.
  • And brother to Mandi Gosling.
  • Started acting at age 13.
  • He was a child star on the Disney Channel's "The Mickey Mouse Club".
  • Then moved on to other TV programs like "Are You Afraid of the Dark," and "Goosebumps."
  • His first film role was a Jewish neo-Nazi in "The Believer".
  • Known for "La La Land," "Drive," "The Notebook" and more.
  • His most recent movie is "The Fall Guy".
  • He is currently 43 years old.
